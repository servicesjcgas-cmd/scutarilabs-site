Offline Auto Invoice

In Development

Autonomous Receipt OCR & PDF Invoice Generation. Zero Cloud.

Corporate accounting SaaS platforms mandate uploading every sensitive employee expense and receipt to remote multi-tenant clouds. Offline Auto Invoice executes quantized optical character recognition directly on your mobile device's neural delegate, compiling structured PDF invoices without an active data connection.

Core Features

  • • On-Device OCR Delegate: Extract line items, tax IDs, and totals using local quantized machine vision with zero latency.
  • • Air-Gapped PDF Compilation: Generate SHA-256 verifiable, audit-compliant PDF expense reports directly on device storage.
  • • Zero Telemetry: Financial documents never leave the phone. Full compliance with UK GDPR and corporate privacy standards.
