We operate as a data controller in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018). This policy explains how we collect and manage personal data through our website (scutarilabs.com), commercial SDK licensing inquiries, enterprise customer relationships, and email correspondence.

2. Architectural Commitment: Zero-Cloud, Strictly On-Device Software

Our software engineering philosophy is fundamentally privacy-first. ScutariLabs software applications and SDKs (including ScutariCore and Scutari Audio Core) are engineered with an air-gapped, zero-cloud architecture:

  • We do not operate central telemetry ingestion pipelines, cloud databases for consumer audio streams, or remote user tracking servers.
  • Raw audio, sensor telemetry (magnetometer, BLE scans, network probes), and inferenced classifications remain sandboxed in volatile device memory (RAM) and are never transmitted to ScutariLabs.
  • For dedicated disclosures regarding consumer mobile applications, please consult our specific product policies: Travel Safe AI Privacy Policy, RoomClear Privacy Policy, and Stillnet Privacy Policy.

3. Information We Collect Through Business Operations

We collect only limited personal data necessary to conduct legitimate corporate operations:

  • Commercial & Technical Licensing Inquiries: Name, work email address, company name, target operating systems, device fleet estimates, and technical use cases submitted via our licensing inquiry forms or email correspondence.
  • Website Server Logs: Standard HTTP request headers (IP address, user-agent, request timestamp) generated automatically by our hosting infrastructure for network security and DDoS mitigation. We do not use third-party analytics trackers or tracking cookies.
  • Contractual Records: Billing details, corporate registration data, and cryptographic public keys for enterprise partners executing commercial license agreements.

4. Lawful Bases for Processing (UK GDPR Art. 6)

We process personal data under the following lawful grounds:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): To issue evaluation licenses, deliver signed Ed25519 commercial license keys, and fulfill SLA support commitments.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): To respond to B2B business inquiries, safeguard our web infrastructure, and improve SDK documentation.
  • Legal Obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): To maintain statutory accounting and tax records required under UK company law.

5. Data Retention & International Transfers

Commercial correspondence and licensing records are retained for the duration of the commercial relationship plus 6 years for UK statutory tax compliance. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Where corporate data is transferred internationally (e.g., enterprise communication tools), we enforce approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or UK International Data Transfer Agreements (IDTAs).

6. Your Statutory Rights (UK GDPR)

Under the UK GDPR and DPA 2018, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and request data portability. To exercise these rights, contact our Data Protection desk at support@scutarilabs.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
