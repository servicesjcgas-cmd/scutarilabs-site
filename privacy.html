Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026
Developer / Entity: Scutari Labs
Contact: support@scutarilabs.com

1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Applications developed by Scutari Labs ("we", "our", or "us") are built with an offline-first, zero-cloud architecture. We do not collect, transmit, track, or sell your personal data.

2. Sensitive Permissions & Local Sensor Processing

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively for real-time acoustic analysis locally in device memory. Audio streams are never recorded to disk, never streamed to cloud servers, and never reviewed by humans.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Processed strictly on-device to attach coordinates to user-initiated emergency alerts. We do not maintain or upload location histories.
  • Bluetooth & Routing: Used strictly to detect whether connected audio output routing (e.g., wireless earpieces) is active for alert delivery.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Because no user data or voice recordings are uploaded to remote servers, there is no personal data held in cloud databases. Clearing the application's local storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local configuration data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our applications are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.
