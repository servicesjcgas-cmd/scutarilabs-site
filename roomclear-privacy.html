RoomClear Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Developer Entity: ScutariLabs Ltd

RoomClear by ScutariLabs Ltd is a tactical counter-surveillance and bug-sweeping tool. We operate on a strict "Zero-Trust, Zero-Cloud" architecture. 100% of sensor processing occurs locally on your device hardware.

1. Hardware Permissions & Data Handling

  • Camera & Flashlight: Used exclusively for real-time optical lens hunting (retro-reflective strobe) and Edge ML object detection. Optical frames are processed ephemerally in volatile RAM and are immediately destroyed. Zero video or photos are ever written to disk or transmitted to a cloud server.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Used exclusively for the Ultrasonic Analyzer to run a local Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) checking for 18kHz-22kHz acoustic beacons. Audio streams are analyzed in volatile RAM and immediately discarded. No audio is ever recorded, stored, or uploaded.
  • Bluetooth & Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Android OS requires Fine Location permission to scan for Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) frequencies and smart trackers. GPS coordinates are strictly used locally to stamp PDF audit logs if the user manually initiates an export. This data never leaves your device.

2. Network Access

RoomClear is designed to operate in airplane mode. The app's manifest is air-gapped, meaning it possesses zero internet permissions for data transmission. Network access is strictly restricted to Google Play Billing verification for in-app purchases.

3. UK, EU (GDPR) & USA (CCPA) Compliance

RoomClear adheres to privacy-by-design principles. Because no personal data, telemetry, or behavioral tracking is collected or stored on external servers, there is no consumer data to monetize or sell.

4. Contact

For support or privacy inquiries, contact: support@scutarilabs.com
