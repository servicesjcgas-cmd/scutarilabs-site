RoomClear

Tactical counter-surveillance and bug-sweeping tool. 100% on-device processing.

Find hidden cameras in your Airbnb or hotel in 60 seconds. RoomClear leverages your smartphone's native hardware sensors to highlight magnetic, optical, and RF anomalies associated with concealed surveillance devices.

Hardware Level Sweeps

  • Tri-Sensor Sweep: Tap into the native magnetometer, high-contrast IR filters, and a BLE Bloom filter to cross-reference local anomalies against known IoT device signatures.
  • Retro-Reflective Strobe: Active optical lens hunting uses high-frequency LED pulses to highlight the specific light refraction of hidden, offline camera glass.
  • Edge ML Threat Profiler: AR bounding boxes automatically spot and highlight high-risk objects like smoke detectors, alarm clocks, and ventilation shafts.
