RoomClear

Tactical counter-surveillance and bug-sweeping tool. 100% on-device processing.

Find hidden cameras, persistent stalker trackers, and acoustic beacons in your Airbnb or hotel. RoomClear leverages your smartphone's native hardware sensors to hunt threats across optical, magnetic, RF, and acoustic spectrums.

Tactical Modules

  • Edge ML Profiler: AR bounding boxes automatically spot and highlight high-risk hardware like smoke detectors, clocks, and ventilation shafts.
  • Retro-Reflective Strobe: Active optical lens hunting uses high-frequency LED pulses to highlight the refraction of hidden, offline camera glass.
  • Smart Tracker Sweep: Identify persistent Apple AirTag and BLE stalking beacons moving with you.
  • Ultrasonic Analyzer: Run a local Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to detect 18kHz-22kHz acoustic tracking beacons just above human hearing.
  • Audit Log Export: Generate an immutable, cryptographic PDF safety report stamped with your sensor spikes and GPS coordinates.
