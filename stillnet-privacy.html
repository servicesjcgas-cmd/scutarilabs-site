Stillnet Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Developer Entity: ScutariLabs Ltd

Stillnet by ScutariLabs Ltd is a 100% offline local network vulnerability scanner and active threat countermeasure utility. We operate on a strict "Zero-Trust, Zero-Cloud" architecture.

1. Data Collection & Handling

  • Local Network Scanning: Stillnet scans local network topologies and connected devices. All network mapping, packet analysis, and vulnerability threat assessments are processed entirely on-device.
  • Zero Cloud Analytics: No personal data, telemetry, or behavioral tracking is collected or stored on external servers. There is no consumer data to monetize or sell.

2. Network Access Permissions

Stillnet requires network permissions exclusively to scan your Local Area Network (LAN) for vulnerabilities and to apply local countermeasures. The app is otherwise air-gapped and does not transmit your scan logs or device profiles to external servers. Network access is strictly restricted to local functions and Google Play Billing verification for in-app purchases.

3. UK, EU (GDPR) & USA (CCPA) Compliance

Stillnet adheres to privacy-by-design principles. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted off your device, we do not hold identifiable records or tracking profiles.

4. Contact

For support or privacy inquiries, contact: support@scutarilabs.com
