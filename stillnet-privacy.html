Stillnet: Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Stillnet by ScutariLabs is a 100% offline local network vulnerability scanner and active threat countermeasure utility. We operate on a strict "Zero-Trust, Zero-Cloud" architecture[cite: 4, 7].

1. Data Collection & Handling

  • Local Network Scanning: Stillnet scans local network topologies and connected devices. All network mapping, packet analysis, and vulnerability threat assessments are processed entirely on-device.
  • Zero Cloud Analytics: No personal data, telemetry, or behavioral tracking is collected or stored on external servers[cite: 5, 7]. There is no consumer data to monetize or sell[cite: 5, 7].

2. Network Access Permissions

Stillnet requires network permissions exclusively to scan your Local Area Network (LAN) for vulnerabilities and to apply local countermeasures. The app is otherwise air-gapped and does not transmit your scan logs or device profiles to external servers. Network access is strictly restricted to local functions and Google Play Billing verification for in-app purchases[cite: 4, 5].

3. UK, EU (GDPR) & USA (CCPA) Compliance

Stillnet adheres to privacy-by-design principles[cite: 5, 7]. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted off your device, we do not hold identifiable records or tracking profiles[cite: 5, 8].

4. Contact

For support or privacy inquiries, contact: support@scutarilabs.com[cite: 4, 5].
