Stillnet

Your Local Network Vanguard. 100% Offline. Air-Gapped.

Stillnet is a local network vulnerability scanner and active threat countermeasure utility. Designed for absolute privacy, it scans for malicious or vulnerable networks and allows you to deploy legally compliant countermeasures without harvesting your data or serving ads.

Core Architecture

  • Local Network Topology: Rapidly map connected devices and assess local subnets entirely on your device.
  • Offline Vulnerability Engine: Cross-reference discovered nodes against an air-gapped threat database to identify malicious intent without relying on cloud processing.
  • Active Countermeasures: Deploy native security responses to shield your device from identified rogue networks or aggressive tracking.
