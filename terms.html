Applications published by Scutari Labs are intended solely as personal assistive and situational awareness tools. They do not replace professional security services, first responders, or official emergency dispatch operators (such as 999 or 911).

2. UK Legal Disclosures (Companies Act & UK GDPR)

Scutari Labs operates in the United Kingdom. Inquiries regarding statutory notifications or data controller obligations may be directed to our official contact desk at support@scutarilabs.com.

3. EU Digital Services Act (DSA) & GDPR Compliance

Under EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR), users retain statutory rights to request information regarding any data handling. Because our applications utilize local-only processing with zero remote databases, we maintain no identifiable records or tracking profiles.
