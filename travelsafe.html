Travel Safe AI

Your Personal Safety Net. Offline. Private. Always On.

Most safety apps trade your privacy for protection, constantly streaming your location and ambient audio to corporate cloud servers. Travel Safe AI flips the script. Using advanced on-device audio classification, your phone acts as an independent guardian.

Core Architecture

  • Zero Cloud Tracking: Your location and audio data never touch an external server. Everything stays securely sandboxed on your physical device.
  • 100% Offline Capable: No signal? No problem. Core safety triggers and acoustic monitoring run entirely via Edge AI without needing cellular data.
  • Stealth Routing: Dynamic audio-to-haptic switching whispers alerts into your Bluetooth earbuds, or uses silent vibration patterns if none are connected.
